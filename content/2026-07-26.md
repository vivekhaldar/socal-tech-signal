# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · July 26, 2026**<br>
**Events from Monday, July 27 through Tuesday, August 4**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This is a build-and-boundaries week. The strongest programs move past generic AI enthusiasm into the practical questions: how agents should be trusted, how prototypes become working systems, how teams preserve context, and where AI can create measurable value for organizations and communities.

This edition uses one quality threshold across the region. There is no total-event cap and no requirement that Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego have equal counts.

## How this issue was made

Candidates were collected from the prior issue, Luma’s Los Angeles and San Diego discovery pages, Meetup, Eventbrite, Builders & Backers, The Ticket 500, SoCalCyber, dev.events, recurring organizer and professional-chapter calendars, university and accelerator searches, and a fresh open-web sweep. A read-only Gmail search of the project account, `vh@vivekhaldar.com`, found no inbound event submissions within the query scope; Gmail is not authoritative beyond that search.

This issue contains 26 qualifying event occurrences from July 27 through August 4: 10 in Orange County, 6 in Los Angeles, and 10 in San Diego. Sixteen future occurrences from the prior issue survived re-verification, and ten newly discovered occurrences cleared the same editorial threshold. Every selected date, time, city, in-person status, and visible access condition was checked against a canonical event or official organizer page on July 26, 2026 between roughly 9:44 and 9:51 a.m. PT.

The filter rejected generic networking lists, virtual-only events presented as local, stale or duplicated listings, thin lead-generation programs, candidates without an announced program, and listings with unresolved schedule or registration conflicts. Claude 101 was dropped after its external registration page simultaneously described the event as a preview with signups not live and as full; Ship Happens was dropped because its canonical page published a time-zone conflict. InfosecMap returned a 403 response, while the SoCal Python and AI Tinkerers Orange County direct sources also failed during this sweep, so their candidates were checked through other current organizer and discovery sources where possible. Event details and availability can still change—check the linked registration page before traveling.
