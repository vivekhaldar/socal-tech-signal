# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · August 3, 2026**<br>
**Events from Tuesday, August 4 through Tuesday, August 11**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This is a week for turning experiments into operating practice. The strongest rooms move from live prototypes and research papers into the harder work of durable AI adoption, accountable community tools, government-market entry, and systems that keep delivering after the demo.

This edition uses one quality threshold across the region. There is no total-event cap and no requirement that Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego have equal counts.
