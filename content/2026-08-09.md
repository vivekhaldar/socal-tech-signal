# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · August 9, 2026**<br>
**Events from Monday, August 10 through Tuesday, August 18**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week rewards rooms where the work has to survive contact with reality. Builders will push agents into short demos, robots onto the field, AI into cloud and drug-discovery workflows, and security and procurement decisions into operating constraints.

This edition uses one quality threshold across the region. There is no total-event cap and no requirement that Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego have equal counts.

## How this issue was made

Candidates were carried forward from the prior issue, then collected from every source in the project registry: Builders & Backers, Luma’s Los Angeles and San Diego discovery pages, The Ticket 500, Orange County AI, AI Tinkerers Orange County, Orange County ACM, Irvine AI, OC CTO Talks, tech night!, SensAI Hackademy, AI LA, Postman developer listings, THE BR-DGE, Pacific Foundry, Broken Data Club LA, Product.ai, SoCal Python, Claude Community Events, DEVx, San Diego Machine Learning, San Diego iOS Developers, San Diego Cyber Group, AWS groups, ISACA chapters, SoCalCyber, Meetup, Eventbrite, Alliance for SoCal Innovation, dev.events, InfosecMap, and a fresh open-web sweep. New official sources—including Sustain SoCal, IEEE San Diego, The IIA, Aquillius, Transition Careers, and Navy Gold Coast—were used only after their current event pages cleared the same verification bar. A bounded read-only inbox search found no direct current submission that passed the filter; no message or draft was created.

This issue contains 30 qualifying event occurrences from August 10 through August 18: 11 in Orange County, 9 in Los Angeles, and 10 in San Diego. Build a Website with AI, LA Agentic AI Meetup, and AI Town Hall were carried forward and re-verified; 27 newly discovered occurrences cleared the same editorial threshold. Orange County AI Office Hours was removed during the Monday publication refresh because it had ended. Events that begin inside the window may continue beyond August 18. Dates, times, cities, in-person status, and visible access conditions were initially checked on August 9, 2026; every retained event page was rechecked on August 10, with final source checks completed through 7:24 a.m. PDT.

The filter rejected generic networking and social lists, virtual-only programs, stale or duplicated pages, thin lead-generation events, and candidates without a sufficiently concrete current program. Specific exclusions included the invitation-gated Irvine executive dinner with recruiting signals and conflicting location/time-zone copy; Product.ai Labs, whose current structured date conflicts with stale July body copy; PitchFest, whose page mixes stale 2025 and 2026 material; Irvine Underground, whose official and secondary pages disagree on venue and end time; AI Mavericks, whose discovery date is stale; and the San Diego JavaScript meetup, whose current presentation remains TBD. The main GRC conference was not listed separately because its public page does not expose exact main-session hours, although the fully timed pre-conference workshop above cleared verification.

InfosecMap returned HTTP 403 or an internal error during regional checks. SoCalCyber’s rendered event cards were inaccessible, but its current API/ICS feed worked for discovery; San Diego Tech Scene blocked direct fetches, so no lead from its search index was included without a canonical-page check. Alliance’s public feed appeared stale through June, broad Eventbrite results were heavily polluted by duplicate hidden-venue training and generic networking pages, and some Luma pages did not render in the browsing view even though their canonical HTML and structured event data remained available. EnergySec’s sales page simultaneously showed the correct future dates and an ended/unavailable registration state, so the listing above treats registration as closed rather than promising access. Event details and availability can still change—check the linked registration page before traveling.
