**Edition · August 24, 2026**<br>
**Events from Tuesday, August 25 through Tuesday, September 1**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week is unusually implementation-heavy: agent reliability, MCP integrations, generative interfaces, on-device models, federated learning, security practice, and hardware infrastructure all have rooms with a real agenda.

This refresh queried the project’s proven source registry, reopened the four carry-forward events that remained upcoming, processed the future-event queue, searched regional organizer and discovery pages, and verified shortlisted occurrences against their canonical pages. The cutoff was fixed at 12:11 p.m. PDT on August 24, 2026. “Newly surfaced” means absent from the prior edition; the source pages do not consistently prove when each listing was first posted.

The result is 40 qualifying in-person or genuinely hybrid event occurrences from August 25 through September 1: 12 in Orange County, 11 in Los Angeles, and 17 in San Diego. One requested September 2 event appears separately under “Later on the radar.” Events that begin inside the window may continue beyond September 1.

Vendor-hosted, approval-gated, and community events survived only where the program was concrete and the access limits are explicit.
