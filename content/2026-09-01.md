## How this issue was made

This refresh queried every source in the project registry, reopened the two September 1 events that remained in progress or upcoming, processed the queued September 2 event, searched regional organizer and discovery pages, and verified shortlisted occurrences against current canonical pages. The cutoff was fixed at 9:01 a.m. PDT on September 1, 2026. “Newly surfaced” means absent from the prior edition; the source pages do not consistently prove when each listing was first posted.

The result is 23 qualifying in-person or genuinely hybrid event occurrences from September 1 through September 8: eight in Orange County, eight in Los Angeles, and seven in San Diego. Events that begin inside the window may continue beyond September 8.

The same filter rejected virtual-only sessions, generic mixers without a technical or operating program, stale or speculative dates, duplicated listings, thin lead-generation pages, and events whose current schedule could not be resolved. Vendor-hosted, approval-gated, sold-out, and community events survived only where the program was concrete and the access limits are explicit. The queued Executive Whiskey Tasting was omitted because its canonical page still did not expose a verifiable current request state.

Several source limitations remain. Eventship’s index and SD Tech Scene blocked direct automated access even though their individual listings were available through live search; Infosecmap blocked the sweep; Product.ai’s old calendar route and Data Con LA’s event route returned not-found responses; and the SensAI calendar rejected the request. IEEE vTools exposed inconsistent date metadata, the VeeamON registration route restricted automated detail reads, and USC BME published contradictory registration language. Dates, inventory, venues, and approval states can still change—check the linked registration page before traveling.
