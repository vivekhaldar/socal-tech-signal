# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · September 14, 2026**<br>
**Events from Tuesday, September 15 through Tuesday, September 22**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

The hard part is shifting from getting AI to produce something to knowing whether it works. This week pairs design-first coding and hands-on agent workflows with long-context research, software verification, and physical-system demos; SoCal Deep Tech Week and San Diego Design Week add unusually concrete reasons to cross county lines.

One quality threshold across all three regions: no event cap, no regional quotas. All times below are Pacific; multi-day events that begin during this window may continue afterward.

## How this issue was made

Prepared September 14, 2026, with a refresh cutoff of 1:09 p.m. PDT and source checks continuing through 1:15 p.m. The off-cycle coverage window starts the next full day and ends the second following Tuesday. Every event in the latest published September 1 issue had ended before the cutoff, so none carried forward; the future-event queue contained no unprocessed event for this window.

This issue contains 45 distinct events: 10 in Orange County, 17 in Los Angeles, and 18 in San Diego. Editor’s picks and drive-worthy highlights repeat their regional entries and are counted once. TiE’s summit and embedded Survivor competition are combined; Qualcomm’s program and component sessions are counted as one event. STARWEST starts September 20 and runs beyond this issue’s window through September 25.

Discovery checked the project source registry, organizer calendars, Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite, dev.events, professional chapters, universities, and fresh web results. The repository had no open event-submission issues. Dates, locations, programs, and access notes were checked against individual event pages, using public structured event data where dates were absent from extracted page text. A visible registration route is not a guarantee of admission or inventory, and prices marked unstated were not inferred to be free.

Some sources were degraded: Infosecmap, the Alliance calendar, USC Viterbi, and parts of IEEE blocked direct reads; the old Product.ai, Data Con LA, and OWASP OC routes failed; the SensAI calendar rejected access; and SoCalCyber and Ticket 500 exposed little usable calendar text. Eventship blocked direct fetching, but selected individual pages were readable through web retrieval or the organizer’s own site. HanaHaus’s Eventbrite ticket state was rate-limited, and The Pitch Pit’s ticket inventory could not be read. These limits are called out rather than treated as verification.

Excluded candidates included virtual-only workshops, generic mixers, thin promotional listings, broken canonical pages, and unresolved schedule or venue conflicts. The Dream AI Hackathon appeared in an LA discovery feed but its canonical venue is San Francisco. Several Ticket 500 event links returned 404, and two Deep Tech summit pages had unresolved location or schedule discrepancies. Known access caveats remain visible on included events: closed registration at Space Beach, waitlists for hardware and site-visit programs, hidden addresses, pending venues, and conflicting end-time wording for Qualcomm and the Swift meetup. Recheck the linked organizer page before traveling.
