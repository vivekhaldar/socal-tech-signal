# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · September 14, 2026**<br>
**Events from Tuesday, September 15 through Tuesday, September 22**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week’s strongest rooms are built around people learning together: design-first AI coding in Orange County, a clinical-audio journal club at USC, and a long-context paper discussion in San Diego. Community demos, reading groups, and open work sessions carry the rest of the list, with free admission at 25 of the 27 events.

All times are Pacific. Free admission is preferred; the two paid programs list their prices and available discounts.

## ⭐ Editor’s picks

### [Gen AI: Design is the New Code](https://www.meetup.com/acm-oc/events/316132346)

**Wednesday, September 16 · 6:30–8:30 p.m. · Irvine · Free · Request to join on Meetup · Parking validated**<br>
Google engineer Elise Ewing will introduce the Elephant Goldfish Model: build a rich design context, then use fresh AI sessions to challenge its assumptions before implementation. The ACM and IEEE program at Knobbe Martens is a particularly good fit for engineers trying to preserve architectural judgment while delegating more code to agents.

### [PRAXIS Journal Club: What Can AI Hear in Clinical Audio?](https://luma.com/4hg55zom)

**Thursday, September 17 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. · Los Angeles · Free · Registration open**<br>
Lily Wu leads a PRAXIS journal club on what audio language models can capture beyond transcripts, handling long clinical recordings, and evaluating them responsibly. It is open to everyone at USC’s Leavey Library, room 202I, with no technical background required.

### [DEVx Paper Club: Position Embeddings and Long Context](https://luma.com/devx-paper-club-sep-17-2026)

**Thursday, September 17 · 7:00–9:00 p.m. · San Diego · Free · Registration open · New venue**<br>
Tom McTavish leads a discussion of RoPE, NoPE, and QK-Norm, connecting position encoding to long-context behavior and model architecture. DEVx has moved this session to the seminar space off the lobby at 11988 El Camino Real; check the new address even if you have attended before.
