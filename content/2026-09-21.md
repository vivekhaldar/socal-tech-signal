# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · September 21, 2026**<br>
**Events from Monday, September 21 through Tuesday, September 29**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week rewards people who want to see systems work under pressure: screened AI demos in Irvine, an LLM-security capture-the-flag in Marina del Rey, and six dual-use teams showing counter-UAS, autonomy, cooling, and sensing technology in San Diego. The rest of the field is unusually deep in app security, agent reliability, open source, developer study, and technical founder programs.

All times are Pacific. Access is not flattened into a generic “open” label: waitlists, approval gates, hidden addresses, closed registration, audience restrictions, and prices that organizers do not publish are called out directly.

## How this issue was made

Revised September 21, 2026, with canonical-page checks through 1:25 p.m. PDT. The nominal off-cycle window is September 22–29; the displayed coverage begins September 21 because five events retained from the prior issue were still upcoming at the refresh cutoff, and one additional same-day event was newly verified.

The selection contains 36 distinct events: one Worth the Drive pick, 11 in Orange County, 13 in Los Angeles, and 11 in San Diego. The three editor’s picks repeat their regional listings and count once. A separate Worth the Drive region was added to the generated site and email so the Riverside summit does not disappear from the published output or get mislabeled as Los Angeles or Orange County.

Prices and registration states came from organizer pages, rendered ticket controls, structured ticket data, and live registration forms. “No event fee listed” means the Meetup organizer configured no fee but did not explicitly label the occurrence free; “price not stated” and “price not published” are deliberate, not guesses. Closed registration, waitlists, approval gates, hidden venues, age or employer restrictions, and paid parking remain visible.

Discovery covered every proven source in the registry plus fresh Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite, professional-chapter, university, accelerator, and broader-web searches. Infosecmap returned 403, SoCalCyber’s embedded event list was stale, Eventship blocked rendered verification, and some Eventbrite routes rate-limited; none of those indexes was treated as final verification. No registrations, applications, waitlists, or organizer forms were submitted.
