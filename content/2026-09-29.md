# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · September 29, 2026**<br>
**Events from Wednesday, September 30 through Tuesday, October 6**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week pairs live systems with practical scrutiny: nine medtech startups demo in Irvine, screened AI builders compare working code in Venice, and OWASP puts AI-generated code through security feedback in Santa Monica. San Diego adds LLM finetuning study, applied-AI building, climate research, and a showcase of local prototypes.

All times are Pacific. Free seats, paid tiers, approval gates, waitlists, hidden venues, and unpublished prices are shown separately. Some programs disagree on their ending time; those discrepancies remain visible so you can plan accordingly.
