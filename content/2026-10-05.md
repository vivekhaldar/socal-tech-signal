**Edition · October 5, 2026**<br>

Three later events are listed separately; the three editor’s picks repeat their regional entries and count once. Rendered Eventship ticket controls verified VibeCraft, 1 Million Cups, and AI Hub; DAMA and SIEMposium registration pages supplied current ticket tiers and fees.
