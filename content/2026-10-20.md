**Edition · October 20, 2026**<br>

## How this issue was made

Prepared October 20, 2026, for October 21–27. The edition contains 41 distinct in-window events: 14 in Orange County, 12 in Los Angeles, 14 in San Diego, and 1 under Worth the drive. 3 later events appear separately; the three editor’s picks repeat their regional entries and count once.

Discovery combined the source registry, fresh regional searches, university calendars, professional chapters, and community indexes. Canonical event pages established each selected occurrence’s date, physical location, and program. Public ticket controls and structured event data resolved admission details where available; missing prices were never treated as free admission. Vendor promotions, online-only programs, closed registrations without an actionable waitlist, and unresolved date or city conflicts were excluded. Smaller schedule and venue discrepancies are stated explicitly.

The research and validation record is in [the dated sweep notes](../docs/sweeps/2026-10-20.md).
