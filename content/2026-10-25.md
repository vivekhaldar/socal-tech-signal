## How this issue was made

Prepared October 25, 2026, for October 28–November 3. The edition contains 36 distinct in-window events: 11 in Orange County, 12 in Los Angeles, 12 in San Diego, and 1 under Worth the drive. One later event appears separately; the three editor’s picks repeat their regional entries and count once.

Discovery combined the source registry, fresh regional searches, university calendars, professional chapters, and community indexes. Canonical event pages established selected dates, physical locations, and programs. Public ticket controls resolved prices and access requirements where available; missing prices were never treated as free admission. Cancelled events, online-only programs, generic networking, closed registrations without an actionable waitlist or standby option, and unresolved date conflicts were excluded. Smaller schedule or address discrepancies are stated explicitly.

The research and validation record is in [the dated sweep notes](../docs/sweeps/2026-10-25.md).
