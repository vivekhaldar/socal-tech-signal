### [UCI: Learning-Assisted Inference in High Dimensions](https://ics.uci.edu/event/learning-assisted-inference-in-high-dimensions-wasserstein-two-sample-testing/)

**Thursday, November 5 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall, Room 6011**<br>
Zhenhua Lin presents max-sliced Wasserstein two-sample testing, using learned projections and neural witness functions to compare high-dimensional distributions. The research combines sample splitting with valid statistical inference while avoiding expensive repeated resampling. Pick this for statistical learning and evaluation methodology; it runs at the same time as the mechanism-design talk in Room 4011.

### [UCI: Compensation and Budget-Feasible Mechanism Design](https://ics.uci.edu/event/compensation-design-and-budget-feasible-mechanism-design/)

### [UCI: Security, Privacy, AI, and the Future of Naming](https://ics.uci.edu/event/beyond-resolution-security-privacy-ai-and-the-future-of-naming/)
