# SoCal Tech Signal

**Edition · November 15, 2026**<br>
**Events from Monday, November 16 through Sunday, November 22**<br>
AI, engineering, security, and founder events across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

This week gets below the interface: GPU inference pipelines, robotic motion, incident-response decisions, and the materials that could compute differently. Bring a real problem to a workshop or use a research seminar to test the assumptions behind your next build.

All times are Pacific Standard Time (PST). Registration details were checked November 15 and can change. Approval, eligibility, and deadlines appear with the listings; an application is not a confirmed seat.

### [UCI: Rating Games with Strength-Dependent Tie Probabilities](https://ics.uci.edu/event/rating-competitors-in-games-with-strength-dependent-tie-probabilities/)

**Thursday, November 19 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall 6011**<br>
Harvard statistician Mark Glickman models a draw as a third outcome whose probability depends on competitor strength, rather than treating it as half a win and half a loss. The talk covers Bayesian dynamic models, approximate Newton–Raphson posterior updates, and evaluation on correspondence-chess data. Choose this for statistical ideas relevant to rankings, matchmaking, and game systems. Meet in UCI's Donald Bren Hall 6011.
