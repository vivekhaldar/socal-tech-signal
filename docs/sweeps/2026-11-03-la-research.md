### Verification method

Read each canonical page in full. For Luma, fetched the public HTML with `curl -Ls -A 'Mozilla/5.0'`, parsed `__NEXT_DATA__.props.pageProps.initialData.data`, and checked `event.start_at`, `end_at`, `timezone`, `registration_availability`, `ticket_info`, and each ticket's type, cents, approval, membership restriction and disabled state. Converted UTC dates with `zoneinfo.ZoneInfo('America/Los_Angeles')`. Free claims are backed by explicit `type: free` / `is_free`, not a null price.

Rendered Chrome checks confirmed AI Cafe's free public standard tier, No Code Girl's $15/$30 enabled tiers with approval, and the hackathon's LA-seat request form. The form accepts requests, not guaranteed admission; it was not submitted. The research recording failed during encoding; DOM reads and a contact sheet were available, but no complete research recording is claimed.
