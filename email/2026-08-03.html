Three to circle.

The strongest reasons to rearrange the week.

Editor’s pick 01

OC CTO Talks x EvoNexus Irvine: Making AI Stick Beyond the Pilot ↗

Thursday, August 6 · 5:30–8:00 p.m. · Irvine · Free

Senior leaders from Autodesk, Microsoft Innovation Hub, and Vortexa AI will compare the organizational and engineering choices that separate durable AI systems from stalled pilots. The agenda includes a focused panel and audience Q&A rather than a sequence of product pitches.

Editor’s pick 02

San Diego Claude Impact Lab: Transparency and Accountability ↗

Thursday, August 6 · 6:00–10:00 p.m. · San Diego · Registration required · 3 spots remained at verification

Teams will turn a prior community conversation about who sets AI’s rules into working local prototypes, then demo what they built. It is an unusually concrete bridge between responsible-AI discussion and shipped artifacts, and non-developers are explicitly welcome.

Editor’s pick 03

LA Agentic AI Meetup hosted by Pinecone ↗

Tuesday, August 11 · 5:00–7:00 p.m. · Playa Vista · Registration required · Work email requested

Pinecone is convening engineers, founders, and agent builders for member demos and implementation-focused conversation spanning RAG and agentic workflows. The low-key format is designed around seeing what local builders are actually making rather than hearing sales presentations.
