Editor’s pick 01

OC AI Builders Lightning Hackathon ↗

Monday, August 17 · 5:00–8:00 p.m. · Irvine · Free · Registration open

A 50-minute build sprint followed by two-minute demos makes this the week’s sharpest build-to-show loop in Orange County: agents, apps, workflow automation, evals, and developer tools all qualify, while pitches do not. Builders should bring a laptop and arrive ready to make something working alongside the Orange County AI, Nebius, and EvoNexus communities.

Editor’s pick 02

Robot Soccer Cup Hack Day ↗

Friday, August 14 · 9:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. · Venice · Free · Registration open

AI LA and Qualcomm are supplying miniAuto robots and a full build day spanning Arduino/Linux, Edge Impulse, and computer vision before a 32-team tournament. It is the strongest Los Angeles pick for engineers who want hardware, edge AI, and a real performance test in the same room.
