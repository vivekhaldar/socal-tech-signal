Editor’s pick 01

Intro to Generative UI for Adaptive Experiences ↗

Thursday, August 27 · 6:30–9:00 p.m. · Fullerton · RSVP open · Price not stated

This technical session moves past chat boxes into interfaces that assemble themselves from model output. Expect tool calling, structured outputs, MCP, component catalogs, and demonstrations using the Vercel AI SDK and OpenAI Apps SDK—one of the week’s clearest looks at how agent behavior becomes a usable product surface.

Editor’s pick 02

AIAI Los Angeles: Generative AI + Agentic AI Summits ↗

Wednesday, August 26 · 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m., followed by happy hour · Downtown Los Angeles · $595 individual / $495 each for teams of three or more · Tickets available

One pass covers parallel generative- and agentic-AI tracks at the Conrad Los Angeles. The program gets specific about keeping agents on rails, production evaluations, Google Gemini workflows, autonomous cloud security, and what it takes to move agent systems from a demo into operations.

Editor’s pick 03

Machine Learning over Networks: From Fundamentals to Applications ↗

Monday, August 31 · 4:01–5:30 p.m. · San Diego · Free · In person with hybrid access

IEEE distinguished lecturer Tamer ElBatt will connect communication-efficient federated learning to Fed-Sophia, second-order state synchronization, MIRA for multi-task LLM fine-tuning, digital-health prediction, and multi-tier IoT intelligence. The technical depth and span from algorithms to deployed edge systems make this San Diego’s strongest research room of the week.
