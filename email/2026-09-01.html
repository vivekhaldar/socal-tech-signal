Editor’s pick 01

Session Harness with Ethan ↗

Saturday, September 5 · 2:00–5:00 p.m. · Newport Beach · Free · Registration open

Bring a Claude or Codex project and put a working harness around the session. The promise is concrete: leave with a project setup that helps the model retain context, use tokens more deliberately, and improve from one working session to the next.

Editor’s pick 02

Semantic Search, RAG, and What Comes Next with Pinecone ↗

Thursday, September 3 · 6:00–9:00 p.m. · Santa Monica · RSVP open · Price not stated

Gavin Johnson will demonstrate a Pinecone-backed application, then move from conventional multimodal RAG into Pinecone Nexus and precompiled context for agents. The combination of implementation detail and a concrete next-generation retrieval architecture makes this the week’s strongest open engineering talk.
