Editor’s pick 01

Gen AI: Design is the New Code ↗

Wednesday, September 16 · 6:30–8:30 p.m. · Irvine · Free · Request to join on Meetup · Parking validated

Google engineer Elise Ewing will introduce the Elephant Goldfish Model: build a rich design context, then use fresh AI sessions to challenge its assumptions before implementation. The ACM and IEEE program at Knobbe Martens is a particularly good fit for engineers trying to preserve architectural judgment while delegating more code to agents.

Editor’s pick 02

PRAXIS Journal Club: What Can AI Hear in Clinical Audio? ↗

Thursday, September 17 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. · Los Angeles · Free · Registration open

Lily Wu leads a PRAXIS journal club on what audio language models can capture beyond transcripts, handling long clinical recordings, and evaluating them responsibly. It is open to everyone at USC’s Leavey Library, room 202I, with no technical background required.
