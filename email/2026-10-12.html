UCI: Item Response Theory for Environmental Exposure Data ↗

Mount Sinai’s Shelley H. Liu shows how item response theory can put cumulative chemical exposures on a common scale across studies that measure different chemicals. The talk covers mixture models, regularized differential item functioning, and exposure calculators, with PFAS and plasticizer examples. Pick it for statistical modeling and the practical problem of harmonizing heterogeneous datasets.
