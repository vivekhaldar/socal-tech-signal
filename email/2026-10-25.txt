UCI: Bayesian Feature Selection in High Dimensions
Thursday, October 29 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall, Room 6011
Illinois statistics professor Feng Liang presents Variational Automatic Relevance Determination, a method that decides whether each feature contributes zero, linear, or nonlinear behavior to a sparse additive model. The talk includes coordinate-descent implementation and comparisons on simulated and real data. Choose it for statistical learning, interpretable feature selection, and efficient inference.

UCI: Record Linkage for Streaming Data
Tuesday, November 3 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall, Room 2011
Colorado State’s Andee Kaplan presents a Bayesian approach to matching duplicate records across messy data sources as new files arrive. Recursive updates and a Markov chain Monte Carlo sampler aim to retain the inference quality of batch methods at much lower compute cost. Pick it for data engineering, entity resolution, and statistical methods for evolving datasets.
https://ics.uci.edu/event/record-linkage-for-streaming-data/
