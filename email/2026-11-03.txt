UCI: Learning-Assisted Inference in High Dimensions
Thursday, November 5 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall, Room 6011
Zhenhua Lin presents max-sliced Wasserstein two-sample testing, using learned projections and neural witness functions to compare high-dimensional distributions. The research combines sample splitting with valid statistical inference while avoiding expensive repeated resampling. Pick this for statistical learning and evaluation methodology; it runs at the same time as the mechanism-design talk in Room 4011.
https://ics.uci.edu/event/learning-assisted-inference-in-high-dimensions-wasserstein-two-sample-testing/

UCI: Compensation and Budget-Feasible Mechanism Design
Thursday, November 5 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall, Room 4011
Ioannis Anagnostides studies how to pay independent contributors while respecting a budget and encouraging truthful participation. The algorithms and game-theory talk covers incentive design and efficient approximation under subadditive valuations. Choose it for the mathematical foundations of marketplaces and distributed collaboration; it conflicts with the statistics seminar in Room 6011.
https://ics.uci.edu/event/compensation-design-and-budget-feasible-mechanism-design/
