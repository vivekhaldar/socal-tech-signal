UCI: Prediction, Confidence and Calibration in Deep Learning

UCI: Statistical Attribute Alignment for Black-Box Generative AI
Thursday, November 12 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Irvine · Donald Bren Hall 6011
Princeton's Jason Klusowski studies how to align synthetic-data attributes when a generative model is available only through queries. UCI: Avi Wigderson on the Value of Errors in Proofs
Friday, November 13 · 11:00 a.m.–Noon PST · Irvine · Calit2 Auditorium
Avi Wigderson traces classical and modern definitions of proof, showing how interaction, randomness, and errors lead to surprising computational power. The journey connects Turing's work to the 2020 MIP*=RE breakthrough and its consequences for complexity theory, mathematics, and physics. UCI's announcement explicitly says no special mathematical background is required. Choose this for foundational computer science explained through the changing notion of a proof.
https://ics.uci.edu/event/the-value-of-errors-in-proofs-a-fascinating-journey-from-turings-1936-r-re-to-the-2020-breakthrough-of-mip-re/

Caltech: The Quest for Representational Universals
Tuesday, November 10 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Pasadena · Caltech, Annenberg 105
MIT's Phillip Isola examines common structures that emerge in learned representations. One result aligns vision and language embeddings up to rotation without paired data; another finds nearby solutions to downstream tasks in the weight neighborhoods of large models. The research asks whether increasingly large neural networks converge on simpler, more universal geometries. Choose this for a technical account of representation learning rather than an introduction to AI tools.
