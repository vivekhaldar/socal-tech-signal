The strongest reasons to rearrange the week.

Editor’s pick 01

UCI Builders: Fall Startup Demo Day ↗

Tuesday, November 24 · 7:00–8:30 p.m. PST · Irvine · Free · Public applications · Approval required

Four startup teams pitch and demonstrate their work, take live questions, and receive judges' feedback at UCI's Merage Auditorium, SB1 1200, 4293 Pereira Drive. UC Irvine Builders and Sigma Eta Pi welcome the public, but the active registration requires host approval: request a place before traveling. Presenting ventures and judges have not been announced. Choose this for live founder work and feedback, not an assumption that every team is building AI.

Editor’s pick 02

Caltech AI Alignment: E-values for AI Safety and Neuroscience ↗

Monday, November 23 · 5:00–6:00 p.m. PST · Pasadena · Free · Registration open · Student-focused

Anirudh Gajula, a Stanford ICME PhD student and SPAR researcher, introduces e-values: an alternative to p-values that permits stopping or continuing an experiment without inflating type-I error. The talk connects the mathematics to streaming data, multiple hypotheses, AI safety, and neuroscience. Caltech AI Alignment hosts in Broad 100, 360 South Wilson Avenue. Interested students need no specific technical background; pizza and boba are provided.

Editor’s pick 03

UCSD: Tensor PCA via Local Markov Chains ↗

Monday, November 23 · 2:00–3:00 p.m. PST · La Jolla · UC San Diego, CSE 4258

Stanford's Aniket Das examines how local Markov chains reach the algorithmic threshold for spiked-tensor PCA. The theory seminar connects Glauber and Langevin dynamics, annealing, and ergodic averaging with polynomial-time recovery from random initialization. Joint work with Tselil Schramm addresses the gap between what information permits and what efficient algorithms can recover. Meet in UCSD CSE 4258; choose this for a mathematically focused research session.

Monday, November 23 · 11:00 a.m.–Noon PST · Riverside · Winston Chung Hall 205/206 · University research audience

UCR: AI, Data Centers and the Electrified Grid ↗

UCLA's Rajit Gadh examines how AI can coordinate data-center demand, EV fleets and charging, renewable generation, batteries, and microgrids. Research and deployment examples connect flexible loads with lower costs, emissions, and improved reliability. Meet at UC Riverside's Winston Chung Hall, rooms 205/206. The published audience is faculty, staff, and undergraduate and graduate students; this is a specialist electrical-engineering seminar.

Orange County

 2

Monday, November 23 · 11:00 a.m.–Noon PST · Irvine · UCI ISEB 1010

UCI: Engineering Stem Cell Fate ↗

Quinton Smith explores engineered microenvironments that control induced pluripotent stem-cell differentiation. Mechanical and architectural niches connect to gastrulation, germ-layer specification, and models of the placenta, vasculature, and liver. This chemical and biomolecular engineering seminar links material design with biological development. Meet in the Interdisciplinary Science and Engineering Building, room 1010.

Rainfall Ventures partner and former INTOO USA CEO Yair Riemer discusses what makes a founder stand out, how investors recognize potential before the numbers prove it, and what operating a company teaches that investing alone cannot. UCLA's student Global Entrepreneurship Society hosts in Bunche Hall 1209B, 11282 Portola Plaza. Tuesday, November 24 · 4:00–5:00 p.m. PST · Pasadena · Caltech, Annenberg 105

Caltech: Interpretability for Safety and Scientific Discovery ↗

UC Berkeley's Emma Pierson presents a method for interpreting foundation-model representations and applies it to model safety and scientific discovery. The question is how to make powerful but opaque models more useful without ignoring the risks their opacity creates. This Caltech AI Colloquium meets in Annenberg 105 and is the week's most direct research option for builders working on model understanding.

Bring a laptop, an AI-assisted creative project, or a work in progress for peer questions and feedback at 310 Coffee, 11625 Washington Place. The organizer has posted this specific post-Thanksgiving occurrence; recheck for holiday changes before traveling.

 4

Monday, November 23 · 11:00 a.m.–12:15 p.m. PST · La Jolla · UC San Diego, EBU2 479

UCSD: Soft Robotic Manipulation through Tunable Adhesion ↗

Explore soft grippers that change adhesion and friction through smart materials, subsurface stiffness, and pressure modulation. A decade of research connects those mechanisms to handling objects with different weights, sizes, curvature, and surface textures, with applications in advanced manufacturing. The mechanical and aerospace engineering seminar meets in Engineering Building Unit 2, room 479.

Monday, November 23 · Noon–1:00 p.m. PST · La Jolla · Comer Commons · Free RSVP listed · In-person option

UCSD: Microcapsules and Prodrugs for Tumor Treatment ↗

Thomas Molley presents a platform pairing embolic exclusion with prodrug generation for hypervascular tumors. The research connects enzyme-containing microcapsules, local oxygen and drug production, hydrogel design, and enzyme engineering. UCSD's Cancer Therapeutics Training program lists a physical session at Comer Commons alongside a separate stream. This is a specialist biomedical-engineering research talk; the university's ticket metadata lists a free RSVP.
